# PROJECT LITTLE UNIVERSE — GARMENT & TAILORING STANDARDS
## Physical Engineering, Seam Architecture, and Growth Longevity Specifications

---

## 1. Physical Engineering Mandate

All children's garments produced under The Threador Atelier must comply with the physical engineering standards detailed below. Garments failing any individual tolerance are rejected at the final master tailoring inspection.

## 2. Seam & Construction Architecture

### 2.1 The 2.5-Inch Hidden French Growth Seam
The primary innovation of The Threador Kids is the **Adaptable French Seam System**:

```
                       CROSS-SECTION OF 2.5" EXPANSION SEAM
                       
        Garment Exterior Fabric (Silk / Velvet / Brocade)
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                                                                │ 
        Primary Hand-Felled Seam (Line A)                       │
   ──────────────────────────────────────x──────────────────────┘
                                         │
        Internal Expansion Reserve (2.5 Inches Folded Silk)
        ┌────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────┐
        │                                                       │
        │   Folded Seam Reserve (Line B)                        │
        └───x───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
            │
        Pure Mulberry Habotai Silk Enclosure Chamber
        (Envelopes all raw fiber edges; zero abrasive thread burrs)
```

- **Mechanism:** The internal seam reserve is stitched with a release-chain basting thread (Line A). When the child enters a growth spurt (typically 12–18 months after initial fitting), a parent or local tailor can snip the release chain to immediately expand the garment circumference by up to 2.5 inches (6.35 cm) without unravelling the structural foundation (Line B).
- **Hems:** Skirts, kurtas, and trousers feature a 2.0-inch blind catch-stitched hem buffer that can be dropped down twice (1.0 inch each time).

### 2.2 Seam Types Permitted
- **Enclosed French Seams:** Required for all bodice side seams, armhole joins, and skirt panels.
- **Hong Kong Finish:** Permitted on heavy velvet and winter wool coat interior edges, bound in 100% Habotai silk bias tape.
- **Prohibited Seams:** Standard 3-thread overlock, 4-thread overlock, and chainstitch with raw interior edges are strictly prohibited from touching child skin.

## 3. Stitching & Thread Tolerances

| Parameter | Specification | Tolerance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stitch Density** | 14 to 16 stitches per inch (SPI) | ±1 SPI |
| **Sewing Thread** | 100% Long-Staple Giza Cotton or Spun Silk Thread | Zero polyester monofilament |
| **Needle Gauge** | Size 65/9 or 70/10 Ball-point needles (prevent fiber shearing) | Micro-fine only |
| **Seam Tension** | Balanced low-tension (prevents puckering on fine organza) | Tested at 45° bias pull |
| **Stress Point Reinforcement** | Bar-tacked armholes, pocket joins, and split plackets | 28-stitch micro bar-tack |

## 4. Hardware & Closure Protocols

1. **Buttons:**
   - 100% natural freshwater mother-of-pearl, carved buffalo horn, or brass.
   - All buttons must withstand a minimum pull-off tension of **90 Newtons (20.2 lbf)** for 10 seconds (ASTM F963-17 / EN 71-1 compliance).
   - Buttons on garments for children under 4 years must be secured with backer buttons or heavy cross-shank thread anchors.

2. **Zippers:**
   - Concealed nylon-coil zippers with rounded teeth.
   - Protective silk guard flap installed beneath the entire zipper length to prevent direct contact with skin or undergarments.
   - Plastic teeth or metallic sharp-edge zippers are forbidden on collars and necklines.

3. **Cords & Drawstrings:**
   - Functional waist drawstrings are prohibited on garments for ages 2–6 (strangulation hazard mitigation per CPSC 16 CFR Part 1120.3).
   - Elasticated waistbands must be stitched down with three channels of parallel elastic to prevent rolling.

## 5. Laundering & Fabric Longevity Benchmarks

- **Colorfastness to Washing:** Grade 4.5 minimum (AATCC Test Method 61).
- **Colorfastness to Perspiration:** Grade 4.5 minimum (AATCC Test Method 15).
- **Dimensional Stability:** Maximum 1.5% shrinkage after 5 gentle dry-clean cycles or cold silk-handwash cycles.
- **Pilling Resistance:** Grade 4 minimum on Martindale Abrasion Test after 10,000 rubs.
