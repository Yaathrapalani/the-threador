# PROJECT LITTLE UNIVERSE — PHYSICAL PRODUCT SAFETY STANDARDS
## Mechanical, Chemical, and Flammability Compliance Specifications

---

## 1. Regulatory Compliance Framework

Every garment produced under The Threador Kids collection undergoes stringent physical testing and chemical analysis to ensure full compliance with international child safety regulations:

- **United States:** Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act (CPSIA 2008), 16 CFR Part 1500, 16 CFR Part 1610 (Flammability).
- **European Union:** General Product Safety Directive (2001/95/EC), EN 71-1 (Mechanical and physical properties), EN 71-2 (Flammability), EN 71-3 (Migration of certain elements), REACH Regulation (EC No 1907/2006).
- **India:** Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) IS 9873 (Safety Requirements for Toys and Children's Garments).

### 2.1 Fastener Retention Protocol
- **Mother-of-Pearl & Horn Buttons:** Must be cross-stitched using 6-ply spun silk thread with a minimum of 16 passes through 4 holes, reinforced on the reverse side with a 10 mm clear Habotai silk backing disc.
- **Tension Test:** Subjected to a calibrated 90-Newton constant pull for 10 seconds. Any loosening or thread failure constitutes batch rejection.

### 2.2 Drawstring & Strangulation Hazard Elimination
- In compliance with **ASTM F1816-97** and **16 CFR Part 1120.3**:
  - Garments designed for sizes 2T through 12 have **zero functional drawstrings** at the hood or neck area.
  - Waist ties on dresses and capes must be permanently tacked to the back side-seams with a maximum free length not exceeding 3.0 inches (75 mm) when untied.
  - Cape closures utilize quick-release safety magnetic clasps engineered to decouple under 5.0 lbs (22 N) of lateral pull.

## 3. Chemical Safety & Toxicology Limits

All dyes, mordants, finishes, and metallic threads are screened by independent third-party laboratories (SGS / Intertek):

| Toxicological Parameter | The Threador Maximum Threshold | Industry Standard Limit (CPSIA / REACH) | Testing Methodology |

## 4. Flammability Performance

- Children's ceremonial robes and multi-layered gowns are evaluated under **16 CFR Part 1610 (Standard for the Flammability of Clothing Textiles)**.
- **Natural Flame Retardancy:** 100% pure Mulberry silk and organic English wool possess natural self-extinguishing characteristics with high Limiting Oxygen Index (LOI ~23–25%) and high ignition temperatures (>400°C), vastly superior to synthetic polyesters and acetates that melt into liquid droplets.
- All fabrics achieve **Class 1 (Normal Flammability - Flame spread time > 7.0 seconds)** without the use of toxic halogenated chemical flame retardants.
