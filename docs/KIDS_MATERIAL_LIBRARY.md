## Certified Natural Fibers, Weaving Traditions, and Pediatric Sensation Profiles

---

## 1. Material Philosophy & Safety Certifications

Every textile utilized in The Threador Kids collection must satisfy two non-negotiable criteria:
1. **Sensory & Pediatric Purity:** Zero allergenic finishes, zero formaldehyde resins, and zero carcinogenic azo dyes.
2. **Third-Party Certification:** All foundational base fabrics must hold **OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I** (certified safe for infants and toddlers under 3 years) and **GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard)** certification.

## 2. Core Textile Profiles

### 2.1 Mulberry Silk Organza
- **Origin:** Karnataka & Jammu sericulture clusters, India.
- **Composition:** 100% Pure Mulberry Silk (*Bombyx mori*).
- **Weave:** Plain weave, 12 mm (momme) weight.
- **Tactile Quality:** Weightless, crisp, airy float with natural structural memory that does not wilt in humid ceremonial environments.
- **Breathability Index:** High (Air permeability: 185 cm³/cm²/sec).
- **Sensory Sensation:** Frictionless, cool to the initial touch, warms rapidly to child body temperature.
- **Applications:** Layered gowns, flutter sleeves, ceremonial dupattas, flower girl skirts.

### 2.2 English Cotton Velvet
- **Origin:** Heritage weaving mills in Lancashire, UK.
- **Composition:** 100% Organic Long-Staple Pima Cotton pile and ground.
- **Weave:** Cut warp pile, 310 gsm weight.
- **Tactile Quality:** Deep plush, velvety pile with calming tactile feedback. Non-rigid and supple.
- **Breathability Index:** Medium-High (Natural 100% cotton base prevents perspiration buildup common in synthetic velvet).
- **Sensory Sensation:** Soothing, deeply soft against skin; natural thermal insulation for winter celebrations.
- **Applications:** Royal bandhgalas, winter wedding capes, festive coats, and tailored shorts.

### 2.3 Gossamer Bamboo-Silk Tulle
- **Origin:** Sustainable bamboo-viscose and organic silk co-spinners.
- **Composition:** 70% Organic Bamboo Viscose, 30% Pure Mulberry Silk.
- **Weave:** Micro-hexagonal open mesh, 24 gsm weight.
- **Tactile Quality:** Cloud-like volume without stiffness. Replaces standard harsh scratchy nylon netting completely.
- **Breathability Index:** Maximum (Air permeability: > 450 cm³/cm²/sec).
- **Sensory Sensation:** Featherlight; zero prickle or neck redness even after 10 hours of continuous active play.
- **Applications:** Voluminous underskirts, capes, veil accents, and theatrical sleeves.

### 2.4 Varanasi Handloom Kadhwa Zari Brocade
- **Origin:** Varanasi pit-loom master weavers, Uttar Pradesh, India.
- **Composition:** 85% Katan Silk ground, 15% Pure Silver-gilded zari wire.
- **Weave:** Traditional *Kadhwa* (each motif is individually engraved on the loom using supplementary weft shuttles with zero loose floating threads on the reverse).
- **Tactile Quality:** Supple, fluid drape with regal dimensional gleam.
- **Sensory Sensation:** All metallic wires remain strictly on the outer face; the reverse is entirely smooth Katan silk, lined with Habotai silk to prevent any skin contact.
- **Applications:** Heirloom Nehru waistcoats, ceremonial sherwanis, festive lehenga borders.

### 2.5 Bengal Matka Handspun Silk
- **Origin:** Malda and Murshidabad weaving villages, West Bengal, India.
- **Composition:** 100% Handspun Matka Mulberry Silk.
- **Weave:** Handloom textured plain weave, 95 gsm weight.
- **Tactile Quality:** Subtle slubbed texture reminiscent of fine linen, with the softness and temperature-regulating properties of pure silk.
- **Breathability Index:** Very High.
- **Sensory Sensation:** Naturally porous; absorbs up to 30% of its weight in moisture without feeling damp to the child.
- **Applications:** Festive kurtas, summer ceremonial tunics, comfort churidars.
